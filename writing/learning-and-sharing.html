Why I built Phi, GAT, and Himma

Hasan Alseba · Updated 13 November 2026 · 1 min read

Each of these websites began with something I or other students needed for studying.

Phi: studying with my teammates

My teammates and I needed one place to collect physics and astronomy resources, problems, and explanations. I built Phi for that purpose. I wanted it to explain concepts in ways that helped us understand the problems we were working on.

GAT: preparing for an approaching exam

My GAT exam was getting close, and my peers were preparing too. I built the GAT website so we could study for it. It has mathematics and verbal practice in English, with mistake review and exam simulation.

Himma: a school mathematics project

My mathematics teacher asked us to make a creative project that would help students. I chose to build Himma, an interactive mathematics website with explanations, worked examples, and tests.

Teaching alongside my own preparation

I also help my astronomy teammates with mathematics and physics concepts, and I wrote a spherical astronomy handout for them. I am now training 10th-grade NSMO physics students with my school while attending the elite training camp for astronomy and astrophysics.
