Teaching / THE NOTEBOOK

Making what I learn useful to others

Hasan Alseba · Based on my learning journey · 2 min read

Alongside studying for Olympiads, I have been building resources for other students.

Helping with spherical astronomy

Spherical astronomy became a subject in which I could support my teammates. I helped students understand the material and put together a handout that develops the geometry from its mathematical foundations.

The handout moves from spheres and great circles to coordinate rotations, spherical-triangle equations, and astronomical coordinate systems. You can find it in my teaching resources, alongside a folder of recorded spherical astronomy lectures.

Building for school

In parallel with Olympiad preparation, I worked on an interactive GAT website, Himma for my school, and Meran, a website for Qudurat mathematics. I have also been working on Phi, a physics and astronomy website.

These projects connect my interest in learning with my interest in making educational tools. My GitHub profile collects the source repositories.

A notebook that can grow

This website brings those parts of my work together: what I have studied, what I have built, and what I can share. As I continue learning, I want the collection to grow with me.
