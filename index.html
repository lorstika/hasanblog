About

I started learning physics and mathematics on my own in seventh grade. After finding out about Olympiads, I wanted to take part. My studying was often messy and unplanned, but I kept going.

I later joined the astronomy Olympiad programme, studied Fundamental Astronomy, and began helping my teammates with spherical astronomy. I wrote a handout on the subject, which you can read below.

I’m interested in symmetry and its connection to conservation laws. I find it astonishing that symmetry can tell us why momentum or energy is conserved. My study note on symmetry →
