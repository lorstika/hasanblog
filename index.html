About

I began studying physics and mathematics on my own in seventh grade. Discovering Olympiads gave that curiosity a direction, even when my self-study was messy and unstructured.

Much of the work I share here connects mathematics to astronomy: describing the sky through geometry, rotations, and coordinates. I studied Fundamental Astronomy, wrote a spherical astronomy handout, and helped my peers understand the subject.

I’m especially interested in symmetry and its connection to conservation laws. The way a mathematical idea can lead to conservation of momentum and energy is what I find so astonishing. A short note on that connection →
