ABOUT

Learning, independently.

PhysicsPure mathematicsAstronomy

I began studying physics and mathematics in seventh grade. Discovering Olympiads gave that curiosity a direction, even when my self-study was messy and unstructured. I kept going through Calculus I–III, mechanics, and oscillations.

Astronomy brought the mathematics into focus. Through the Mawhoob Olympiad pathway and later NSMO, I joined—and, after a period away, returned to—the Saudi national astronomy team. Along the way, I studied Fundamental Astronomy and helped my peers make sense of spherical astronomy.

Here I collect that journey, the educational websites I build, and the notes I share. I’m looking for opportunities to learn through research, work with others, and deepen my understanding of the physical world.
