About

I began studying physics and mathematics on my own in seventh grade. Discovering Olympiads gave that curiosity a direction, even when my self-study was messy and unstructured. I continued exploring both subjects independently, gradually building a deeper understanding of their ideas.

Astronomy brought the mathematics into focus. I studied Fundamental Astronomy and helped my peers make sense of spherical astronomy.
